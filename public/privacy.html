Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 · wreckly.ca

This Privacy Policy explains how Wreckly (a sole proprietorship registered in Ontario, "Wreckly", "we") collects, uses and shares personal information when you use wreckly.ca. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

1. What we collect

  • Request details you give us: your name, email, phone number (optional), postal code, the vehicle (year, make, model, VIN if provided) and the part you need.
  • If you are a yard: your yard's name, contact person, email, phone, address, and the types of vehicles you carry.
  • Payment information: deposits are paid through Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us the payment status, amount and your email.
  • Technical and usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel (cookies or similar technologies).
  • Emails you send us.

2. How we use it

  • To contact yards on your behalf and tell you what they have and at what price.
  • To process your deposit and reserve a part.
  • To email you about your request and reservation.
  • To contact yards about requests and to invite them to join Wreckly.
  • To run, secure and improve the site, measure our advertising, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

  • Yards. When we ask yards about your part, we share the vehicle and part details, and your general area. After you pay a deposit, we give the yard that holds your reserved part your name and contact details so you can pick the part up. Before that, we do not give yards your name or contact details.
  • Service providers that help us operate: Stripe (payments), Supabase (database hosting), Resend (email delivery), Netlify (website hosting), Google (analytics) and Meta (advertising measurement). They may process information on our behalf under their own privacy terms.
  • Legal. If required by law or to protect rights, safety or against fraud.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and advertising

We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how people use the site and how our ads perform. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use your browser's privacy controls, or manage ad preferences in your Google and Meta accounts.

5. Where information is stored

Our providers may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

6. How long we keep it

We keep requests, reservations and payment records as long as needed for the purposes above and for tax and legal reasons. We keep yard contact details until the yard asks us to remove them.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@wreckly.ca. Some information must be kept for legal or accounting reasons. A yard can ask us to stop contacting it at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any of our emails.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls and trusted providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children

Wreckly is not directed at children, and you must be at least 18 (or the age of majority where you live) to reserve a part.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with an updated effective date.

11. Contact
