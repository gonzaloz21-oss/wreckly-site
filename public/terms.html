2. How It Works

  • Request: the buyer submits the part needed, the vehicle, their location and their contact details.
  • Availability and price: Wreckly asks nearby Yards whether they have the part and at what price. Each Yard sets its own price and confirms its own availability.
  • Reservation: if a Yard has the part, the buyer may reserve it by paying a deposit equal to 10% of the Yard's quoted price (the "Deposit") through our payment provider.
  • Contact release: after the Deposit is paid, Wreckly gives the buyer the Yard's contact details and address, and gives the Yard the buyer's name and contact details.
  • Balance: the buyer pays the remaining balance (the quoted price minus the Deposit) directly to the Yard when they pick up the part. Wreckly does not collect the balance.
