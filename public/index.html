FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I find used auto parts near me in Ontario?

Send Wreckly a free request with the part, year, make and model. We contact auto yards, junkyards and scrap yards across Ontario and email you who has the part and the price, so you skip the phone calls.

Which areas do you cover?

We contact yards across Ontario, Canada, including the Greater Toronto Area, Hamilton, Kitchener-Waterloo, London and Ottawa, and plan to expand.

What if the part is wrong or the yard doesn't have it?

If the yard doesn't have the part when you arrive during the reservation period, or it doesn't match what was described, you get your deposit back. See section 4 of the Terms for details.

Does the deposit add to the price?

No. The deposit is part of the price: you pay 10% to Wreckly to reserve the part and the other 90% to the yard at pickup.

Who gives the warranty on a used part?

The yard. Wreckly is an intermediary and gives no warranty on parts. Ask the yard for its warranty in writing before you pay the balance.

Do you sell used parts yourselves?

No. Parts are sold by independent auto yards and recyclers. Wreckly finds the part, confirms the price and holds it for you with a 10% deposit.

I run a yard. How do I join?

It's free: there is no sign-up fee and no monthly fee. Tell us about your yard and we'll send you requests.
