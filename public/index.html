How it works

Four steps. Zero phone tag.

Tell us the part

Year, make, model and the part you need. Takes two minutes.

We call the yards

We contact nearby yards and ask: do you have it, and at what price?

Reserve it

If a yard has it, you pay a 10% deposit to hold the part and get the yard's contact and address.

Pick it up

Go to the yard, check the part, and pay the remaining 90% directly to them.

Pricing

Free to ask. Pay only when we find it.

No cost to request

Submitting a request is free. If no yard has your part, you pay nothing.

The 10% deposit

The yard sets the price. You reserve the part with a deposit of 10% of that price, and the deposit counts toward the price.

Example: alternator quoted at $200 → you pay $20 to Wreckly to reserve it and $180 to the yard at pickup.

Wreckly is an intermediary

The yard sells the part.
We save you the calls.

Parts, condition and warranty are between you and the yard. Ask the yard for its warranty in writing before you pay the balance. Read the full Terms.

FAQ

Questions

Which areas do you cover?

We currently work with yards in Ontario, Canada, and plan to expand.

What if the part is wrong or the yard doesn't have it?

If the yard doesn't have the part when you arrive during the reservation period, or it doesn't match what was described, you get your deposit back. See section 4 of the Terms for details.

Does the deposit add to the price?

No. The deposit is part of the price: you pay 10% to Wreckly to reserve and the other 90% to the yard.

Who gives the warranty?

The yard. Wreckly is an intermediary and gives no warranty on parts.

I run a yard. How do I join?

It's free. Tell us about your yard and we'll send you requests.
